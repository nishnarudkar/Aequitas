TERMS AND CONDITIONS:

1. EFFECTIVE INTEREST RATE AND FLOATING RESET:
The effective interest rate shall be floating and reset monthly at the sole discretion of the Lender without prior notification. Additional administrative and processing charges of 4.5% of loan value shall be deducted upfront from loan disbursement.

2. PREPAYMENT AND FORECLOSURE PENALTY:
Early prepayment or foreclosure of the loan within 24 months of disbursement shall incur a mandatory penalty charge of 6% on the total original loan principal amount.

3. DEFAULT, LATE INTEREST, AND RECOVERY:
Default in EMI payment by more than 3 days shall attract penal interest at 36% per annum compounded monthly. The Lender reserves the right to initiate recovery proceedings without notice, attach guarantor personal assets, and contact employer HR departments.

4. GUARANTOR LIABILITY:
The Guarantor agrees to be jointly and severally liable for the full loan amount, interest, legal costs, and recovery fees in the event of any default by the Borrower.

5. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION:
Governed by laws of India and subject to courts in Kolkata, West Bengal.
