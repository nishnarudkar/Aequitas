LEAVE AND LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Leave and License Agreement is made at Mumbai, Maharashtra this 1st day of January 2025, by and between:

LICENSOR: Mr. Rajesh Kumar, residing at Flat 101, Sea View Apartments, Bandra West, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400050.
LICENSEE: Ms. Priya Sharma, residing at Flat 202, Sunshine Heights, Andheri East, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400069.

WHEREAS the Licensor is the absolute owner of Flat No. 402, Sunshine Apartments, Bandra West, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400050 (hereinafter referred to as the "Licensed Premises").

NOW IT IS HEREBY MUTUALLY AGREED AND DECLARED AS FOLLOWS:

1. PURPOSE AND PERIOD:
The Licensor hereby grants to the Licensee permission to use and occupy the Licensed Premises for residential purposes only for a period of 11 (eleven) months commencing from 1st January 2025 to 30th November 2025.

2. LICENSE FEE:
The Licensee shall pay to the Licensor a monthly license fee of INR 35,000 (Rupees Thirty-Five Thousand Only), payable on or before the 5th day of each calendar month. Late payment shall attract a penalty of 2% per month on the outstanding amount.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT:
The Licensee has deposited a sum of INR 150,000 (Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand Only) as refundable, interest-free security deposit. The Licensor agrees to refund the deposit within 90 days after vacating the premises, subject to deductions for structural damage or unpaid electricity bills.

4. LOCK-IN PERIOD AND TERMINATION:
There shall be a strict lock-in period of 11 months during which the Licensee cannot terminate this agreement. If the Licensee vacates prior to the completion of the lock-in period, the entire security deposit of INR 150,000 shall be forfeited by the Licensor. The Licensor reserves the right to terminate this agreement at any time by giving 7 days prior written notice without assigning any reason.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS:
The Licensee shall be responsible for all minor and major structural repairs, plumbing, electrical fixtures, and painting of the premises upon vacating.

6. INSPECTION AND ENTRY:
The Licensor or his authorized representative shall have the right to enter and inspect the Licensed Premises at any time without prior notice.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION:
This agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and subject to the jurisdiction of the courts in Mumbai, Maharashtra under the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999.
